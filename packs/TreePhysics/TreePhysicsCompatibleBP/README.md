## 树木物理：连滚带伐

为“树木物理”添加对其他 Add-Ons 附加包树木的支持。

只使用原版树木时无需此扩展包。

### 依赖

**必须与“树木物理”的行为包和资源包同时启用。**

### 支持范围

仅支持以常规方式生成、结构符合常规树木特征的附加包树木，且少量树木仍可能被遗漏。

### 已知限制与风险

- 仅对启用后新生成区块中的树木有效，并会增加世界生成压力。
- 过于庞大的树木可能在物理化时造成严重卡顿甚至游戏崩溃。
- 极少数类似树木的自然木质结构（如村庄房屋）可能被误判并物理化。

## Tree Physics: Timber Tumble

Adds support for trees from other Add-Ons to Tree Physics. 

It is not needed when playing with vanilla trees only.

### Dependencies

**The Tree Physics behavior pack and resource pack must both be enabled.**

### Supported Trees

Only Add-On trees with conventional generation and structure are supported, and a small number may still be missed.

### Known Limitations and Risks

- Only trees in chunks generated after this expansion is enabled can be recognized, at the cost of additional world generation work.
- Excessively large trees may cause severe performance loss or crash the game when they become physical.
- In rare cases, tree-like natural wooden structures, such as village houses, may be misidentified and become physical.
